Prepare before the flight

  1. Open this app while you still have Internet.
  2. Use your browser's Add to Home Screen / Install option.
  3. Open every game once so assets are ready.
  4. Test with airplane mode on and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth off.
  5. Save your nickname and squad before boarding.
Important: This app itself makes no network, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, hotspot or mobile-data connection. It cannot disable your phone radios. Follow Ryanair crew instructions and do not switch on radios for this game.
